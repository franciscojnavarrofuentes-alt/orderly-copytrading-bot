# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Be respectful
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be considerate of differing viewpoints
- Accept constructive feedback graciously

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing others' private information

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainer privately.
